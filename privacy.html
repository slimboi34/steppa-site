Steppa

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Steppa is a step-counting game for iPhone. No data leaves your device. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking. We (the developer) do not receive any of your data.

Health data

With your permission, Steppa reads your steps, walking and running distance, flights climbed and active energy from the Apple Health app. This data is read only to show you your own stats, charts, level, boss fights and quests. Steppa never writes anything to Health, and never shares, sells or uploads Health data. You can turn off access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Steppa.

Motion & Fitness

While the app is open, Steppa uses your iPhone's pedometer (Core Motion) to show a live step count. The readings stay on your iPhone.

What is stored on your phone

Your settings (daily goal, units, reminders) and your game progress (when you started, completed quests, which scores you have last sent to Game Center) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage and in an App Group shared with Steppa's widget so the widget can show your numbers. They are included in your normal iPhone backups under Apple's control. Deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Steppa schedules local notifications on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, Steppa can submit your level, your boss count, your best daily step count and your unlocked badges to Apple's Game Center, which is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Steppa does not receive any of it. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent and the game works exactly the same.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No network requests of our own: Steppa works fully offline.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, because we never receive it.

Children

Steppa is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be posted here. Questions? See Support.
