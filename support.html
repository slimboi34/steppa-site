Frequently asked questions

Steppa shows 0 steps.

Steppa reads steps from the Health app. Open Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Steppa and make sure Steps, Walking + Running Distance, Flights Climbed and Active Energy are on. Apple keeps your answer private, so Steppa can't tell if access was refused. The Fight and Today screens have buttons that take you straight to Settings or Health.

How do steps turn into XP and damage?

Every 10 steps is 1 XP, and every step is 1 point of damage to the boss you're fighting. Completed daily quests give 50 XP each, beating a boss gives 200 XP and beating the weekly raid boss gives 500 XP. Only steps since you started your adventure count.

Why did my count differ from Health?

While the app is open Steppa also listens to the iPhone's pedometer for a live count, and shows the higher of the two. Health combines your iPhone, Apple Watch and other sources, so totals can briefly differ until Health catches up.

How does the streak shield work?

Hit your daily goal to extend your streak. If you miss a day and your shield is ready, the streak survives (the missed day just doesn't add to it). The shield then recharges for 7 days. Today never breaks a streak while it's still in progress.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Leaderboards and badges in Game Center are optional. Sign in through the Settings app if you want them. Everything else works the same without it.

What is Steppa Pro?

Steppa is free to play. Steppa Pro unlocks the full boss roster, weekly raids, all three daily quests, the streak shield, 30-day and 1-year trends, personal bests and every badge. Plans are yearly (with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers), monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase. XP and levels are the same for everyone. See the subscription terms.

How do I cancel, restore or manage Pro?

Open Settings › Steppa Pro in the app: Manage Subscription opens Apple's subscription page, and Restore Purchases brings Pro back on a new iPhone (use the same Apple ID). You can also cancel in the iOS Settings app under your name › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I start over?

Yes: Settings › Restart my adventure resets your level, bosses and quests. Your steps in Health are never touched.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
