Steppa Pro: subscription terms

Steppa is free to download and play. Steppa Pro is an optional upgrade. Purchases are made through the App Store and handled entirely by Apple; Steppa has no accounts and no servers.

What Pro includes

The full boss roster and its tougher Mk versions, weekly raid bosses, all three daily quests, the streak shield, 30-day and 1-year trends, personal bests and every badge. Live step counting, the Today screen, XP and levels, the first three bosses, one daily quest, 7-day trends, widgets, the Siri shortcut, reminders and Game Center leaderboards are free. Your XP and level are the same on every plan, and boss progress carries on from your real step history when you upgrade.

Plans

  • Steppa Pro Yearly: £14.99 a year, with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers.
  • Steppa Pro Monthly: £2.99 a month.
  • Steppa Pro Lifetime: £29.99, paid once. It does not renew.

Prices are for the United Kingdom and may differ in other regions or change over time. The price you pay is always shown in the app before you confirm.

Billing and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase, or when a free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless they are cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period, at the price of that plan. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

Managing or cancelling

Manage or cancel your subscription in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Steppa. You can also open Settings › Steppa Pro › Manage Subscription inside the app. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Restoring purchases

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, open Settings › Steppa Pro › Restore Purchases (the paywall has the same button) while signed in to the same Apple ID.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
